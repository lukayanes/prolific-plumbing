WHAT IS A FAUCET & LEAK REPAIR SERVICE?

Faucet and leak repair covers everything from dripping faucets and running fixtures to leaks in supply lines, shutoff valves, and pipes behind walls or under slabs. Quick repair helps prevent water damage, mold, and higher water bills.

At Prolific Plumbing, we diagnose the source of the leak, explain options clearly, and complete clean, reliable repairs. Whether it’s a small drip or a hidden leak causing damage, we’ll fix it fast and help you avoid repeat issues.

Best for:

Dripping faucets, under-sink leaks, leaking valves, pipe leaks, water stains, and unexplained high water bills.

Common calls:

Kitchen/bath faucet drips, leaking toilets, pinhole leaks, slab leaks, low pressure, corroded fittings.
